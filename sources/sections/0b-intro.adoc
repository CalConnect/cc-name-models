== Introduction

Personal names are among the most commonly exchanged information on the
Internet. However, the detail of personal names during interchange
often recedes to the lowest common denominator understood by
both sides, which is a plain string.

Such exchange invariably entails the loss of most semantic
structure of a personal name, causing a range off issues from
inappropriate greetings at hotels to failure of name verification at
national borders.

This International Standard facilitates the interchange and interaction
of names between humans and applications without loss of fidelity.
