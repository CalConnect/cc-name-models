
=== Manchu

given name(s) (+ optional family name)

Manchus are called only by their given names.
Manchu clan names ("`hala`") are not used with their personal names,
but occasionally a family name is used.

[example]
Tulišen

[example]
Hiowan Ye (玄烨)

[example]
Aisin-Gioro Ulhicun, linguist. The family name is "Aisin-Gioro".
